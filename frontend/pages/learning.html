Good habits, lower risk

Quick cyber safety tips

Six small checks that can help protect your accounts, money, and personal information.

✓

Verify payment requests

Contact the person through a channel you already trust before approving an unexpected request.

✦

Use unique passwords

Important accounts should not share passwords. Turn on multi-factor authentication where available.

◈

Keep codes private

Never share OTPs, PINs, CVVs, passwords, or recovery codes through a message or call.

↗

Inspect web addresses

Check the full address before entering account details or making a payment.

↻

Install trusted updates

Keep your phone, browser, and apps updated through their official stores and settings.

Ⅱ

Pause when pressured

Urgency and secrecy are warning signs. Stop and verify independently before acting.

Know the warning signs

Common online scams

Recognize the pattern, then choose a safer next step.

Suspicious payment links

Unexpected requests to pay immediately through an unfamiliar link.

Job and task scams

Offers that request an upfront fee, deposit, or sensitive account details.

Fake investment offers

Guaranteed returns, secret tips, or pressure to act before an offer disappears.

Impersonation messages

A supposed friend or organisation asks for secrecy, money, or a verification code.

Phishing messages

A message creates urgency and sends you to a lookalike sign-in page.

Shopping scams

An unrealistic discount or unknown seller pushes payment outside the platform.
