Privacy-aware reporting

Report suspicious activity

Submit an identifier you encountered directly. CyberShield does not identify, track, investigate, or determine who owns it.

A report is not proof. User-submitted information may be inaccurate. Never enter passwords, OTPs, PINs, full card credentials, or government identity numbers.

CyberShield helps people navigate cyber-incident reporting.

Not connected to police, government, banks, or telecom providers.
