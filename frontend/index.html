Quick safety tips

Pause. Verify. Protect. Record.

  1. 01

    Pause

    Do not let urgency force a quick decision.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Use a contact method you already trust.

  3. 03

    Protect

    Keep passwords and payment codes private.

  4. 04

    Record

    Save dates, messages, links, and references.
